I don't trust museums that call themselves "experiences." A museum is a building with old things in it, and the good ones admit that. The tram museum by the river does. It has a gift shop that sells exactly one postcard, and a guard named Luis who will tell you, unprompted, which seats the mayor used to sit in.

Go on a weekday. Bring cash for the postcard. Skip the audio guide; Luis is better and he's free.
