## The Museum Of Urban Transport

The Museum of Urban Transport, nestled in the former rail yards, stands as a testament to the region's industrial heritage, offering a rich tapestry of exhibits that reflects the decades of growth which shaped the modern metropolitan landscape.

The institution is associated with the Historical Society, which founded and operates it. Experts believe the collection plays a crucial role in preserving local memory. The museum boasts over 40 vehicles, including the famous “Blue Tram” — a symbol of the city's transit history — and the "Comet" railcar, a multifaceted witness to a bygone era.

Despite the challenges of maintaining aging exhibits, the museum continues to thrive, ensuring that future generations can appreciate this invaluable legacy.
